# AST — Inventory and Asset

Scope `[C]`: assets in rooms and consumable supplies. Detailed capability is `[A]`; procurement, suppliers and multi-level warehouse are outside the proposed scope. (BA Part 1, pp.7–8.)

No detailed AST FR exists in BA Parts 2–4. Part 4 schedules AST for a later BA part, so implementation would be premature. (BA Part 4, p.37.)

Known touchpoints: HK may report minibar/asset damage and MNT may handle repair, but posting money must follow PendingCharge/FO confirmation rather than direct AST/HK/MNT folio writes. (BR-057; BA Part 4, p.36.)
