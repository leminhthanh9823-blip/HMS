# MNT — Maintenance

Purpose: record maintenance tickets and minimal MVP room blocking; OOO removes saleable inventory through RoomNight, while a small ticket can leave the room saleable. (BA Part 4, pp.17–20.)

FR-MNT-001–008 cover ticket creation/assignment/severity/status, OOO request/approval/release, conflict checking, notifications and compensation PendingCharge. (BA Part 4, p.18.)

Key rules: BR-015, BR-057, BR-110; RC-20 protects simultaneous OOO and booking. OOO cannot be created over an assigned future stay; move/upgrade first. OOS distinction and standard compensation table/Owner threshold are unresolved (`OQ-054`). (BA Part 4, pp.18–20, 30, 37.)
