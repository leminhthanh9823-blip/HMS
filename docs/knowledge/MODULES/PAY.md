# PAY — Payment, Reconciliation, Refund and Cashier Shift

Purpose: make every cash movement traceable to a booking/folio and external evidence, with maker-checker controls. (BA Part 3, pp.23–28; Part 4, pp.20–25.)

FR-PAY-001–010 cover payment capture, unique transfer reference/VietQR, Pending vs Matched, manual bank-statement reconciliation, allocation/Unallocated and void controls. FR-PAY-101–113 cover refund request/approval/payment, original-channel/payer checks, OTA treatment, reversals, cashier shifts/blind count/variance and POS settlement. (BA Part 3, pp.23–24; Part 4, p.22.)

Key rules: BR-011, BR-030, BR-033, BR-037–040, BR-043–046, BR-064, BR-130–131, BR-135–136. A transfer screenshot is never proof; Pending does not satisfy deposit/settlement. Concurrent refund/shift risks are RC-18/RC-19. (BA Part 3, pp.24–28; Part 4, pp.23–25, 29–30.)

Open: payment/refund methods OQ-012; bank format/API OQ-049; pending deadlines OQ-048; one-person approval OQ-040; shift variance and payout API are still unspecified. (BA Part 3, p.40; Part 4, p.25.)
