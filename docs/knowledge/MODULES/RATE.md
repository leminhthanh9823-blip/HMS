# RATE — Rate Plan and Pricing

Purpose: the only pricing engine used by quote, booking, folio and reports. Input is room type/rate plan/dates/occupancy; output is nightly price source, supplements, cancellation policy and deposit rule. (BA Part 3, pp.3–8.)

FR-RATE-001–015 cover Rate Plans, base/weekday/season/date prices, occupancy and supplements, cancellation/deposit rules, discount/override approval, stay restrictions, quote explanation and lifecycle. (BA Part 3, pp.3–5.)

Key rules: BR-017, BR-020–027, BR-030–032, BR-064, BR-120. Missing/zero rate blocks booking. Cancellation, child/extra-bed, discount thresholds and tax remain open through OQ-007–OQ-010, OQ-014. (BA Part 3, pp.5–8, 39–40.)
