# FO — Front Office / Check-in and Check-out

Purpose: manage arrival through departure, including walk-in, room assignment, early/late, move, extension, early departure, undo, pending charges and lodging-declaration export. (BA Part 4, pp.3–9.)

FR-FO-001–017 cover check-in eligibility and guest documents, payment guarantee, early/late, room movement/extension, service charges, checkout/undo, no-show restoration and declaration export. (BA Part 4, pp.4–5.)

Check-in requires appropriate booking, assigned available room, configured cleanliness threshold, guest documents and remaining amount assurance; Manager overrides are audited. Checkout requires pending charges handled and folio balance zero, then room becomes Vacant + Dirty. (BA Part 4, pp.4–9.)

Key rules: BR-004, BR-006, BR-010, BR-017, BR-021–022, BR-032, BR-034, BR-041–043, BR-045, BR-051–057, BR-066, BR-132–133. Open: OQ-006, OQ-008, OQ-018, OQ-047, OQ-051. (BA Part 4, pp.5–9, 36.)
