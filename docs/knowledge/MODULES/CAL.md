# CAL — Reservation Calendar

Purpose: room × date timeline for availability and controlled drag/drop room or date change, with live updates and visible price impact. (BA Part 3, pp.15–18.)

FR-CAL-001–011 cover timeline display/filtering, drag/drop change, conflict/OOO indicators, unassigned/Conflict queue, room assignment/swap and near-real-time refresh. (BA Part 3, pp.15–16.)

Key rules: BR-010–015, BR-017, BR-021, BR-066. Target `[A]`: 30 rooms ×31 days under 2 seconds and updates within 5 seconds. OOO and parent-child behavior depend on MNT/ROOM; OQ-001 and OQ-005 remain open. (BA Part 3, pp.16–18.)
