# RPT — Dashboard and Reports

Scope `[C]`: operational, financial, exception and consolidated multi-property dashboards. Detailed measures are `[A]`. (BA Part 1, pp.6–7.)

No detailed RPT FR exists in BA Parts 2–4. Part 4 schedules it for a later BA part. (BA Part 4, p.37.)

Known measures: Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, gross/net OTA revenue, daily room discrepancy, approval/self-approval exceptions, AR aging and Night Audit daily report. KPI targets require a two-week pilot baseline and Owner approval (`OQ-030`). (BA Part 1, pp.5–6, 34.)
