Purpose: chain-level guest identity/history/blacklist with minimum data collection, duplicate matching and protected identity documents. (BA Part 3, pp.19–23.)

FR-GST-001–011 cover profile/contacts/identity, phone/email duplicate lookup, stay history, blacklist, consent/purpose notice, masked view/unmask audit, document handling and anonymization/export workflow. (BA Part 3, pp.19–20.)

Key rules: BR-060–063, BR-066–067. Blacklist scope/action is not confirmed (`OQ-034`, `OQ-047`); personal-data basis, retention and deletion remain blocking (`OQ-019`), and full-view roles remain OQ-045. (BA Part 3, pp.20–23; Part 2, p.42.)
